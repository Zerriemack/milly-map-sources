# Competing in Daily Fantasy Sports Using Generative Models

Link: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/itor.13344

Discovery method: Citation chasing from DFS research discussions plus targeted search for operations research papers on DFS.

Annotation:  
Summary: This paper frames DFS as a decision problem under uncertainty and uses generative modeling to create realistic outcome samples, then evaluates lineups under contest payout logic.  
Credibility: Peer reviewed journal publication in an operations research outlet, with methods grounded in modeling and optimization.  
Use in my project: It gives academic language for what serious DFS players already do, especially simulation driven evaluation, payout aware objectives, and portfolio construction.  
Limitations: Modeling choices and data assumptions still matter, and the paper may not capture every slate specific detail a practitioner uses.
