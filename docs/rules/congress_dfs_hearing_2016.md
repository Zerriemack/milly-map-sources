# Congress DFS Hearing Document (2016)

Source link: [Congress.gov witness statement PDF](https://www.congress.gov/114/meeting/house/104902/witnesses/HHRG-114-IF17-Wstate-SchoenkeP-20160511-SD006.pdf)

This source is a primary government posted witness statement submitted for a 2016 House hearing connected to daily fantasy sports. It covers how DFS contests were described in a public policy setting, including how operators explained contest structure, consumer protections, fairness questions, and oversight concerns during a period when regulation and legality were under scrutiny. The document is valuable because it gives a traceable policy record you can cite when discussing DFS rules, disclosures, and trust issues instead of relying on forum talk or marketing language. It is useful for students studying digital regulation, anyone researching contest based markets, and DFS players who want a clearer view of the legal and public accountability environment around the games they play. Limitations include age and perspective. It reflects a 2016 snapshot and a witness framing, so it should be paired with current DraftKings rules pages and independent reporting.
