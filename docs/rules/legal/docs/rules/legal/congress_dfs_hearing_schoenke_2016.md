# Daily Fantasy Sports hearing document (Congress.gov, Schoenke, 2016)

Link: [Congress.gov witness statement PDF](https://www.congress.gov/114/meeting/house/104902/witnesses/HHRG-114-IF17-Wstate-SchoenkeP-20160511-SD006.pdf)

This PDF is a primary government posted document connected to a congressional hearing on daily fantasy sports. It captures a formal set of questions and responses about how DFS contests operate, how fees and prize pools are communicated, and what consumer focused safeguards were being discussed during a period when DFS legality and regulation were under close scrutiny. It also provides context on fairness concerns and how an operator described internal controls in a public record.

This source is valuable because it anchors DFS research in a traceable policy document instead of secondhand commentary. Students studying digital regulation, researchers examining contest based markets, and DFS players who want a deeper understanding of the environment around contest rules would find it useful.

Limitations include age and perspective. It reflects a 2016 snapshot and an operator aligned framing, so it should be paired with current DraftKings rules pages and independent reporting.
